⚡ End-to-End Verification Lifecycle

How ApproveHub Works

From applicant requisition submission to cryptographically sealed certificate in 4 deterministic stages

1
Submit & Place Marks

Applicant uploads PDF, chooses approval chain, and positions resizable mark placeholders on real document pages.

2
Review & Authority Sign

Approver signs via smartphone QR touch canvas or sketch pad with cryptographically secured PIN authorization.

3
PyMuPDF Stamping

Engine dynamically bakes signatures, seals, and QR verification codes onto original PDF pages with sub-pixel precision.

4
Instant Verification

Embassies, employers, or colleges scan the QR code to verify authenticity in real-time with zero-trust validation.
