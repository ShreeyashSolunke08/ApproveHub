PyMuPDF Vector Stamping

Digitally embeds signatures, department seals, and verification QR codes right onto original PDF pages with sub-pixel resolution and tamper-evident SHA-256 hashes.

Bank-Grade PIN Security

Brute-force protection automatically triggers temporary account lockouts after 5 consecutive incorrect PIN attempts, combined with strict IP rate limiting.

How ApproveHub Works

From student submission to globally verifiable signed certificate in 4 steps

1
Submit & Place Marks

Applicant uploads PDF, chooses approval chain, and positions mark placeholders.

2
Review & Authority Sign

Approver signs via phone QR or sketch pad and authorizes with their secure PIN.

3
PyMuPDF Stamping

Engine dynamically bakes signatures, seals, and QR verification codes onto the PDF.

4
Instant Verification

Embassies, employers, or colleges scan the QR code to verify authenticity in real-time.

Multi-Device Cloud Architecture & Isolated User Databases

Built for scale, privacy, and continuous 24/7 availability across all your devices

🔒 Individual Data Isolation

Every registered student and faculty member receives a distinct tenant ID in the database. Personal documents, private touch signatures, security PIN hashes, and audit logs are strictly isolated and never mixed or accessible to unauthorized users.

📱 Multi-Device Real-Time Sync

Just like YouTube, WhatsApp, or Zomato, start an application on your laptop and track or approve it directly on your mobile app. Real-time database synchronisation guarantees instant status updates across smartphones, tablets, and PCs.

🗄️ Cloud Database Engine

Engineered with SQLAlchemy ORM supporting production PostgreSQL on Render / Railway. Fully managed, persistent cloud storage ensures high availability and zero data loss even during heavy multi-user peak submission cycles.
