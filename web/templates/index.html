How ApproveHub Works

From student submission to globally verifiable signed certificate in 4 steps

1
Submit & Place Marks

Applicant uploads PDF, chooses approval chain, and positions mark placeholders.

2
Review & Authority Sign

Approver signs via phone QR or sketch pad and authorizes with their secure PIN.

3
PyMuPDF Stamping

Engine dynamically bakes signatures, seals, and QR verification codes onto the PDF.

4
Instant Verification

Embassies, employers, or colleges scan the QR code to verify authenticity in real-time.
